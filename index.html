Idempotent Transfer Engine

A record-merge job that must land exactly once even when the request is duplicated, the worker crashes mid-transfer, or two transfers race each other. Break it, watch it corrupt, then turn the protections back on and watch it converge.

Cursor resume

Simulation note: concurrency here is modeled as interleaved async ticks in a single browser tab (JavaScript's run-to-completion model), not true parallel threads — the protective mechanisms (dedup key, cursor, lock) are real and behave exactly as they would in a real distributed system; only the "two workers running literally simultaneously" part is simulated rather than physically true.
